Our Story

We chatted a lot and after a long time and a few hesitations, we decided to meet. That was in Paris. We then talked some more, had a delicious dinner, a couple bottles of fine wine, laughed... and somehow neither of us scared the other away. That felt promising.

Since then, we've become partners in adventure, experts at last minute planning, couch buddies and puzzle partners. Through every trip, every ordinary Tuesday, and all our shocking cultural discoveries, we've realised that life is simply better together.

So, we decided to make it official. And here we are.

Should I rent a car?

Uber and Bolt are pretty reliable, but if you're traveling with family or just want extra comfort and flexibility, renting a car is a great option. We'd suggest booking it early so it's cheaper.

Can I bring my kids along for the wedding?

We'd love for you to have a chance to enjoy a little grown up time, but we understand that sometimes the babysitter has other plans. If you don't have anyone to leave your little ones with, bring them along — we will happily welcome them. Just make sure to let us know on your RSVP so we can plan accordingly.
